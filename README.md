# Insurance Policy Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end analytics project on a 5,000-customer insurance dataset, 
covering policy performance, claims, and customer demographics — built in 
SQL, Excel,Tableau
and Power BI.

![Dashboard Screenshot](dashboard/dashboard_screenshot.png)

## Overview
- **Customers analyzed:** 5,000
- **Policies issued:** 5,000
- **Total claim amount:** ₹251M
- **Avg premium growth rate:** 221.61
- **Active : Inactive policy ratio:** 0.5 : 1

## Data
Five linked tables: Customer Information, Policy Details, Claims, 
Payment History, and Additional Fields (agent, renewal status, risk score).

## Key KPIs Answered
- Active / lapsed / terminated policy breakdown
- Average coverage and premium amount by policy type (Life, Health, Auto, Property)
- Active vs. inactive policy ratio
- Age group and gender-wise policy distribution
- Policy type comparison (Health, Life, Property, Auto)
- Year-over-year premium and policy trends (2015–2020+)

## Tools Used
SQL (MySQL) · Excel · Power BI .tableau

## Dashboard Highlights
- 6 KPI cards for at-a-glance metrics (customers, policies, claims, growth rate)
- Interactive slicers for Status, Policy Type, and Age Group
- Pie/donut charts for coverage, premium, and status distribution
- Bar and line charts for demographic and time-trend analysis
